About AACEM Institute

Our Mission & Vision

AACEM Institute is dedicated to providing quality education that empowers students with knowledge, skills, and confidence to succeed in their academic and professional endeavors.

आस्था कम्प्यूटर एजुकेशन मिशन (AACEM) आपका स्वागत करता है। AACEM बिहार राज्य के वैशाली जिले में लखनीपुर रोड़, कारगिल चौक, मौदह, पातेपुर में स्थित है। AACEM विशेष सुविधाओं से वंचित, अल्पविकसित और पिछड़े हुए ग्रामीण क्षेत्रों में कंप्यूटर, माध्यमिक शिक्षा और उच्चतर शिक्षा के क्षेत्र में गुणवत्तापूर्ण शिक्षा प्रदान करता है।

Leadership

Director's Message

Ajit Kumar Singh

Director, AACEM Institute

"Greetings,

I am Ajit Kumar Singh, Director of AACEM Institute, extending a warm welcome to all students, parents, and well-wishers.

AACEM is committed to providing quality computer education and academic coaching in the rural areas of Vaishali, Bihar, where many students—especially girls—often face circumstances that force them to discontinue their education.

Our mission is to transform education through technological expertise and experienced faculty, empowering every student to reach new heights. Our dedicated team of specialist teachers is committed to making students more competent and competitive, ensuring they are well-prepared for both academic and professional success.

We believe that education is the most powerful tool for empowerment, and at AACEM, we strive to make it accessible, relevant, and transformative for every learner."

— Ajit Kumar Singh
Director, AACEM Institute
